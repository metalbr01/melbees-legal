Privacy Policy — Melbees: The Secret of Bees

Effective date: 2026-07-27 · Last updated: 2026-09-06

1. Who we are

Melbees (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is provided by Melbee Games, Brazil. For any privacy question or request, contact melbees.games@gmail.com.

2. Scope

This policy explains what information Melbees handles, how, and your choices. It applies to the Melbees game distributed on Google Play. If the game is later distributed on other stores, this policy will be updated beforehand.

3. Information we handle

Melbees stores the following only on your device to make the game work; none of it is sent to us or to a server:

  • Game progress: solved secrets (your Vault), best times, unlocks and settings.
  • Your age band (13-or-over / under-13), derived on your device from the date of birth you enter. The date of birth itself is used only for that calculation and then immediately discarded — it is never stored or transmitted.
  • Your advertising/analytics consent choice (players 13+).
  • Your chosen language.

We do not collect your name, e-mail, phone number, precise location, contacts, or any account identifier.

The only information that leaves your device is a report you choose to send us from the game's feedback screen, described in section 8. If you never use that screen, nothing is transmitted. The app contains no advertising, analytics or payment SDK, and sends no other data — to us or to third parties.

4. Children and the age gate (COPPA)

Melbees is family-friendly and shows a neutral age screen on first run. Players under 13 are placed in Kids-safe mode, in which:

  • No personal data is collected, and no persistent-identifier analytics are used.
  • Nothing is transmitted. The free-text feedback form (section 8) is not offered: the support screen shows only our e-mail address, so that a responsible adult can get in touch on their own. In this mode the app also assembles no technical block at all.
  • No advertising is shown.
  • No purchases are offered.
  • Account sign-in and social features are not offered.

Players 13 and over use Standard mode. Should personalized advertising or analytics ever exist, they will be used only with consent (off by default), and this policy will be updated beforehand.

5. Advertising

This version does not display third-party advertising. There is no ad SDK in the app. If this changes, we will update this policy and the store listing beforehand.

6. In-app purchases

This version does not offer in-app purchases. If that changes, purchases will be processed by the platform store, not by us — we would not receive your payment details — and this policy will be updated beforehand.

7. Analytics and third-party services

We do not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. The app does not log how you play, does not profile players, and makes no automatic or background network connection. The only network connection the app opens is sending a feedback report that you started, described below.

8. Feedback and support

The game has a Send feedback screen (Settings ▸ Account) and a per-word report on the results screen. They exist so you can tell us about a problem, a wrong word, an idea or a compliment.

8.1 Who can send

Only players in Standard mode (13 or over at the age gate). In Kids-safe mode the form is not shown and the app does not even assemble a submission, even if the screen were reached some other way (section 4).

8.2 When a send happens

Only when you tap “Send”. There is no automatic, scheduled or background sending, and nothing is sent when the game crashes or is closed.

8.3 What is sent

Exactly these items, and nothing beyond them:

  • The category you picked — one of four fixed labels: Problem, Word, Idea, Praise.
  • Your message — the free text you type, up to 5,000 characters. It is the only field you write in, and it reaches us exactly as you wrote it.
  • A technical block (up to 3,000 characters), assembled by the app and shown on screen before you send. It contains only:
    • the app version and build number (e.g. 1.0.0+3) and the build type (release / debug / profile);
    • the platform (e.g. android) and the operating-system version string exactly as the system reports it, truncated to the first 40 characters — on Android this is typically a technical kernel line (Linux 5.10.…), not “Android 14”, and it does not include the device model;
    • the interface language (e.g. pt-BR) and the tap mode chosen in settings;
    • the screen you were on and a list of up to 20 recent actions, each one an internal game code (a card activation, for example) or, when an error occurs, a label from a closed list (range, argument, format, state, unsupported, unimplemented, assertion, type, exception, other). Never the error message, never the stack trace, never anything you typed;
    • for a per-word report only: the run's category and subcategory, the number of secrets (K), the phase, and the word's internal identifier (wordId) — so we know which entry to fix.
  • A fixed anti-spam token shipped inside the app. It is identical on every installation: it identifies the app, not you.

Not sent — because the app never collects them in the first place: your name, e-mail, phone number, address, location, contacts, advertising or device identifier, device model, serial number, file paths, your date of birth, your game progress, your Vault or your best times.

Important: the message field is free text and reaches us as you wrote it. Do not type your name, e-mail, phone number, address or any other personal information there — yours or a child's. We do not need it to fix the game.

8.4 Where it goes, and who has access

The report is sent over HTTPS to a Google Apps Script service operated by Melbee Games, which appends it as one row in a Google Sheets spreadsheet owned by Melbee Games, with access restricted to us. Google acts only as the hosting provider (processor), under its own terms. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to build profiles. We use reports only to fix defects, correct words and decide what to improve.

The spreadsheet row carries no identifier tied to you or your device: there is no account, no device id, and we do not store your IP address. In practice we cannot link a report to a person, nor group two reports from the same person — other than by what is written in them.

8.5 If the send fails

With no internet, or if the service does not answer, the app tells you it failed (it never says “sent” when it did not) and offers two alternatives, both started by you:

  • Send by e-mail — opens your mail app with a short pre-filled message (your message, the app version, the platform, the language, the category and, where relevant, the wordId). You decide whether to send it. If you do, we learn your e-mail address, because it is an ordinary e-mail — and then we can link that message to you. We keep such correspondence as we would any support contact, and you can ask us to delete it by replying to that e-mail.
  • Copy — copies your message and the technical block to the device clipboard. Nothing is sent; you send it however you wish.

8.6 The technical block before it is sent

The information in the technical block lives only in the app's memory while the app is open, is never written to the device, and disappears when the game is closed. In Kids-safe mode this recording is disabled and cleared.

8.7 How long we keep it, and how to delete it

We keep a report row for as long as it is useful to fix what it describes and, in any case, for no more than 24 months from the day we receive it; after that it is deleted.

You can ask us to delete it sooner at any time by writing to melbees.games@gmail.com. Because the rows carry no identifier, we need your help to find yours: tell us the date you sent it (and the approximate time, if you remember) plus an excerpt of what you wrote. We delete the matching rows and confirm by e-mail. Without those two details we have no way to locate your report — not out of refusal, but because the spreadsheet holds nothing that ties a row to a person.

“Delete my data” inside the game erases device data only (progress, settings and the age choice). It does not reach reports you have already sent — use the e-mail route above for those.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Delete my data: Settings → “Delete my data” erases all local game data on the device and returns the app to the age screen. That covers everything the game stores; feedback reports already sent are deleted via the e-mail route in section 8.7.
  • Send nothing: feedback is optional and always started by you. If you never use the feedback screen, we receive nothing.
  • Uninstall: removing the app also deletes the local data.
  • Requests: contact melbees.games@gmail.com.

Melbees is offered first in Brazil, and data handling follows Brazil’s General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13.709/2018 — LGPD), which grants you rights including confirmation, access, correction, anonymization, portability and deletion. Because Melbees keeps game data only on your device, you exercise those rights directly in the app (“Delete my data”); for a feedback report you sent us, use the e-mail address above with the date and an excerpt of the message (section 8.7). The legal basis for handling that report is our legitimate interest in fixing and improving the game from a contact you started yourself (LGPD art. 7, IX). If the game is later offered in the EU/UK or California, the applicable GDPR/CCPA rights and legal bases will be added here beforehand.

10. Data retention

On-device data is kept until you delete it (via “Delete my data”) or uninstall the app; we keep no copies of it. Feedback reports you send us stay in our spreadsheet for no more than 24 months, or less if you ask us to delete them (section 8.7).

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date will change, and for material changes we will provide notice in the app. Features that involve sending data — advertising, accounts, online leaderboards — will only be enabled after this policy has been updated.
