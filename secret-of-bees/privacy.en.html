Privacy Policy — Melbees: The Secret of Bees

Effective date: 2026-07-27 · Last updated: 2026-07-27

1. Who we are

Melbees (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is provided by Melbee Games, Brazil. For any privacy question or request, contact melbees.games@gmail.com.

2. Scope

This policy explains what information Melbees handles, how, and your choices. It applies to the Melbees game distributed on Google Play. If the game is later distributed on other stores, this policy will be updated beforehand.

3. Information we handle

Melbees stores the following only on your device to make the game work; none of it is sent to us or to a server:

  • Game progress: solved secrets (your Vault), best times, unlocks and settings.
  • Your age band (13-or-over / under-13), derived on your device from the date of birth you enter. The date of birth itself is used only for that calculation and then immediately discarded — it is never stored or transmitted.
  • Your advertising/analytics consent choice (players 13+).
  • Your chosen language.

We do not collect your name, e-mail, phone number, precise location, contacts, or any account identifier.

In this version, no information leaves your device. The app sends no data to us or to third parties, and contains no advertising, analytics or payment SDK.

4. Children and the age gate (COPPA)

Melbees is family-friendly and shows a neutral age screen on first run. Players under 13 are placed in Kids-safe mode, in which:

  • No personal data is collected, and no persistent-identifier analytics are used.
  • No advertising is shown.
  • No purchases are offered.
  • Account sign-in and social features are not offered.

Players 13 and over use Standard mode. Should personalized advertising or analytics ever exist, they will be used only with consent (off by default), and this policy will be updated beforehand.

5. Advertising

This version does not display third-party advertising. There is no ad SDK in the app. If this changes, we will update this policy and the store listing beforehand.

6. In-app purchases

This version does not offer in-app purchases. If that changes, purchases will be processed by the platform store, not by us — we would not receive your payment details — and this policy will be updated beforehand.

7. Analytics and third-party services

We do not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. The app makes no network connections to us or to external providers.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Delete my data: Settings → “Delete my data” erases all local game data on the device and returns the app to the age screen. Since nothing is transmitted, this erases everything that exists.
  • Uninstall: removing the app also deletes the local data.
  • Requests: contact melbees.games@gmail.com.

Melbees is offered first in Brazil, and data handling follows Brazil’s General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13.709/2018 — LGPD), which grants you rights including confirmation, access, correction, anonymization, portability and deletion. Because Melbees keeps data only on your device and does not transmit it, you exercise those rights directly in the app (“Delete my data”); you may also write to the address above. If the game is later offered in the EU/UK or California, the applicable GDPR/CCPA rights and legal bases will be added here beforehand.

9. Data retention

On-device data is kept until you delete it (via “Delete my data”) or uninstall the app. We keep no copies, because we receive no data.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date will change, and for material changes we will provide notice in the app. Features that involve sending data — advertising, accounts, online leaderboards — will only be enabled after this policy has been updated.
