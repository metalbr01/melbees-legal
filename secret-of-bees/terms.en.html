A Google Play subscription counts on Windows, and a Microsoft Store one counts on Android, when you sign in with the same Google account on both. When it ends, what this section says for a subscription that stops being active applies. Under Settings → Account, the game shows until when it lasts.

6. Petals and the continue

When the match's Petals run out, the game stops the clock and offers to continue. The options depend on the mode:

  • Watch a rewarded ad: it gives 1 Petal (section 8). Android only: on Windows, a 1-Petal pack is on sale in its place (section 2.1).
  • Buy a pack of 2 or 5 Petals, in Run and in the Daily Challenge. In Walk there is no Petal purchase, for anybody.
  • Premium's free continue, if you subscribe (section 5).
  • Give up, in one tap, always available.

Each Petal received adds time to what is left of the phase and, in the ranked modes (Run and Daily Challenge), adds the same time to your final time, wherever it came from.

Bought Petals:

  • are sold only at that moment, when yours run out. The game has no store;
  • carry Google Play's price, shown beside the Buy button. Without the price, the button does not appear;
  • go into the match at once and are spent in it. They are not kept for another match, have no cash value, cannot be exchanged for money, and cannot be passed to another person, another account or another device;
  • are not a draw: there is no loot box. You know exactly what you are buying, and nothing bought turns into a draw.

If a purchase is waiting for a guardian's approval, the game says it is pending. If the approval arrives while the continue offer is still open, the Petals go into the match. A purchase that is not used on an open continue offer (for example, an approval that arrives after you gave up, a payment that clears only after the match has ended, or the game closing between the charge and the delivery) is not delivered to another match on its own, and Google Play refunds it automatically 3 days after the charge. Before that, if you buy the same pack on a continue offer, the game delivers that already-paid purchase, with no second charge. That automatic refund is Google Play's and applies on Android; on Windows, see section 2.1.
