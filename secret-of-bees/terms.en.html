3. The game is free

  • Every mode is free for everyone: Run, Walk and Daily Challenge. Run and Walk have no limit on matches. The Daily Challenge is one attempt a day per difficulty and language, for everyone.
  • You need to buy nothing and create no account to play. Playing is offline, and your progress is kept on your device only.
  • Without paying you get the free set of categories, all the power cards, the same rewards as any player, the same continue offer in each mode, and the local leaderboard with your own times.

4. The Google account, which is optional

  • An account is not required to play or to buy. It is for the online leaderboard (section 9) and for restoring your subscription on another device (section 5).
  • The account is Google only: there is no e-mail-and-password sign-up. You sign in under Settings → Account → Sign in with Google, or when the online leaderboard asks. The game never asks you to sign in on launch.
  • What we keep from the account is in the Privacy Policy.
  • Deleting the account: under Settings → Account → Delete my account, right away. Without the game installed, through the Delete your account page, and we act within 30 days. This deletes the account, your display name and your runs on the online leaderboard.
  • Deleting the account does not cancel or refund the subscription and erases nothing on your device. Deleting the device data (Delete my data) does not delete the account. They are different actions.

5. Premium, the subscription

Premium is an optional monthly subscription, sold through Google Play, that renews automatically. It does not buy access to any mode: everything section 3 lists stays free. While the subscription is active, it gives four benefits:

  • Online leaderboard: the global leaderboard of each category and the Daily Challenge one (section 9).
  • Premium categories: the categories marked Premium are open.
  • All skins and themes: they are visual and change nothing in play.
  • Free continues: one free 5-Petal continue per Run and one per Daily Challenge, and unlimited continues in Walk.

Premium does not improve your time. In the ranked modes (Run and Daily Challenge), each Petal received in a continue adds the same time to your final time, whether it comes from an ad, a purchase or Premium. The cards and the rewards are the same for subscribers and non-subscribers.

  • 7-day free trial. The price, the period and the trial are shown on the Premium screen as Google Play reports them, before you subscribe. When the trial ends, the subscription starts being charged and renews by itself every month, until you cancel. Google Play decides whether an account can use the trial: an account that has used a trial before may not get another.
  • Subscribing needs no account. To recover the subscription, use Restore purchases, on the Premium screen or under Settings → Account. On another device, the game asks for the Google account.
  • Cancelling: whenever you like, in your Google Play account's subscriptions. Under Settings → Account, Manage subscription opens that page. The game never cancels the subscription for you, and deleting your Melbees account does not cancel it.

When the subscription stops being active:

  • the Premium categories, skins and themes lock again, and you go back to the free set. An equipped skin stops showing, but its record is not erased;
  • the online leaderboard no longer applies to you: you go back to your local times. Times you already sent are not deleted because of it, but no new time is accepted on the online leaderboard;
  • you keep everything on the device and everything you have already earned, such as best times, feats, discovered secrets and settings.

6. Petals and the continue

When the match's Petals run out, the game stops the clock and offers to continue. The options depend on the mode:

  • Watch a rewarded ad: it gives 1 Petal (section 8).
  • Buy a pack of 2 or 5 Petals, in Run and in the Daily Challenge. In Walk there is no Petal purchase, for anybody.
  • Premium's free continue, if you subscribe (section 5).
  • Give up, in one tap, always available.

Each Petal received adds time to what is left of the phase and, in the ranked modes (Run and Daily Challenge), adds the same time to your final time, wherever it came from.

Bought Petals:

  • are sold only at that moment, when yours run out. The game has no store;
  • carry Google Play's price, shown beside the Buy button. Without the price, the button does not appear;
  • go into the match at once and are spent in it. They are not kept for another match, have no cash value, cannot be exchanged for money, and cannot be passed to another person, another account or another device;
  • are not a draw: there is no loot box. You know exactly what you are buying, and nothing bought turns into a draw.

If a purchase is waiting for a guardian's approval, the game says it is pending. If the approval arrives while the continue offer is still open, the Petals go into the match. A purchase that is not used on an open continue offer (for example, an approval that arrives after you gave up, a payment that clears only after the match has ended, or the game closing between the charge and the delivery) is not delivered to another match on its own, and Google Play refunds it automatically 3 days after the charge. Before that, if you buy the same pack on a continue offer, the game delivers that already-paid purchase, with no second charge.

8. Rewarded ads

  • The game shows rewarded ads only, served by Google AdMob. There is no banner and no ad between screens.
  • No ad ever appears by itself. One is shown only when you tap to watch an ad on the continue offer (section 6), and it gives 1 Petal. If you never tap, you will never see an ad. The ad is always labelled as an ad.
  • An ad is not always available. When none is, the game does not show that option. In Walk, with no ad and no Premium, the only option is to give up.
  • The first time an ad is about to be shown, the game asks your age once, with a Skip button of the same weight. Skipping blocks nothing. What the answer is for, and what we keep of it, is in sections 5 and 6 of the Privacy Policy.

9. The online leaderboard and fair play

  • The local leaderboard is free for everyone: your 10 best times on each board are kept on the device.
  • The online leaderboard is a Premium benefit: the global leaderboard of each category and the Daily Challenge one. To send times and see your position, you sign in with the Google account.
  • On the online leaderboard you appear under a name the game generates, which you do not choose, and there is no text field. The public sees only the fifty best marks of each board: rank, generated name and time.
  • The online leaderboard needs the internet and our servers. When they do not answer, the local leaderboard keeps working.

Fair play. Every run sent is re-run on our server, move by move, to confirm the time is real. You may not alter the game, the app or the data it sends, use software to play for you or to make up a run, or exploit bugs in the game to get a time. We may remove results that break these rules.

A purchase waiting for approval shows in the game as pending (section 6).
